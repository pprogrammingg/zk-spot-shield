ZK Spot Shield

Private spot settlement on Solana

I’m Pooyan. I build protocol and payments infrastructure. ZK Spot Shield is a Solana program for private spot settlement.

Problem

  • On Solana every transfer is public: who paid, how much, which wallet — bad for traders hiding inventory, ticket buyers who don’t want seats published, and agents paying APIs all day.

Solution

Prove clean funds, solvency, and a one-time spend on your machine — get a note you can spend without doxing yourself.

On-chain we check the proof and move tokens; the chain sees the trade and spend-tag, not who you are or what’s left.

Why me

Tech lead, 18 years shipping — I need a settle that doesn’t dox the payer, and a year from now I’m still climbing that hill, company or protocol team.

Why now, why Solana

  • HTTP 402 is no longer theoretical. Coinbase shipped x402; Stripe and Tempo shipped the Machine Payments Protocol.
  • Agents will pay per request.
  • Solana is cheap and fast enough that checking a short proof and moving the tokens fits in one transaction.
  • USDC on Solana is already what those 402 rails use.
  • Stripe’s choice of Tempo forces settlement onto a permissioned (invite-only) blockchain. I built on Solana so inclusion doesn’t depend on that list.

Where it is

  • Already runs on a local Solana network: the proof checks out, the vault moves tokens, and a replay or an unregistered tree fingerprint is rejected.
  • The docs are public. It is not live on mainnet.
  • Next: a relayer (pays the fee so your wallet isn’t linked and you don’t need SOL), then a private pay button for those 402 APIs.
